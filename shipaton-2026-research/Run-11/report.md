# Run 11 — validation, instrumentation, and submission story

## Executive summary

Restart Thread now has a proof plan that connects the user promise to the
working build, product evidence, and every selected-award criterion. The primary
demo cut reaches capture in 15 seconds, shows durable local save by 29 seconds,
and reaches the grounded Direct Trace-back value moment by 58 seconds. It then
shows user control, RevenueCat after value, and only conditionally proven Galaxy
and OneSignal behavior within a 1:58 runtime.

The central recommendation is to make **RevenueCat Design criterion 1,
Innovative ideas, the evidence priority**. It is the primary award's first
criterion and therefore tie-break critical. Its proof is not merely the visual
brand: it is the complete local-save-first voice thread, grounded evidence
connector, editable first action, human-confirmed Start, and deterministic
fallback working together on the target device.

Best App for Galaxy and Keep Them Coming Back remain coherent but conditional.
Galaxy needs a live Galaxy listing, adaptive and purchase proof, and a polished
store presentation. OneSignal needs a deployed campaign plus verified Start
value; a send, delivery, or click alone is insufficient. If either gate fails,
remove that award segment and use the time to deepen primary Design proof.

No study, production metric, purchase result, store acceptance, adaptive-device
result, or campaign outcome exists yet. Every such result remains unknown. The
plan deliberately creates predicted attention hypotheses before testing and an
observed first-action and interaction map after consented testing; it does not
claim an eye-tracking or attention heatmap.

## Validation system

The complete plan is in
[validation-plan.md](./validation-plan.md). It separates six evidence phases:
technical truth, formative usability, an unprompted closed-test pilot, signed
release verification, post-launch learning, and submission proof.

### Formative study

The five-participant protocol targets US adults who self-identify with ADHD,
time blindness, interruption sensitivity, or recurring restart difficulty. A
diagnosis is neither required nor recorded as a product outcome. The protocol
tests:

- ten-second store-promise comprehension and first action;
- interruption-time voice or text capture;
- recording start, stop, and durable local save;
- AI identity, evidence grounding, edit, confirmation, and Start;
- microphone, network, transcription, model, source, and stale-action recovery;
- separate microphone permission and cloud-processing consent;
- optional reminder denial and recovery;
- post-value paywall, purchase, restore, grace, and expiry; and
- TalkBack, Switch or Voice Access, keyboard, 200% text, reduced motion,
  magnification, one-handed use, and adaptive-window behavior.

The accepted Run 7 pass, pivot, and stop thresholds are preserved. The stale
“fourth active thread” paywall scenario is replaced by the approved M1 boundary:
the user has already received value and a later cloud request reaches the
configurable free allowance. Owned content and free recovery remain available.

### Attention and interaction evidence

[attention-map-hypotheses.csv](./attention-map-hypotheses.csv) records likely
first fixation, scan order, actions, thumb reach, decision density, and conflicts
for eight critical states. Every row is explicitly a prediction.

After separate research consent, the study build may record screen and control
IDs, normalized coordinates, task-relative timestamps, input mode, outcome,
error, and assistance. The resulting artifact is an **observed first-action and
interaction map**. It may show control choice, timing, wrong turns, and tap
density. It must not be described as gaze, fixation, or eye-tracking evidence.

### Decision rules

Privacy exposure, committed-content loss, reversed negation, incorrect charge
or entitlement, unrecoverable purchase, and critical-path crash or ANR are S0
failures. They stop the affected release candidate. A task-blocking inaccessible
path, misleading cloud consent, hidden free exit, or unsafe deep link is S1 and
also blocks the affected build or award claim.

Product metrics follow causal order. Repair entry and durability before AI;
repair draft quality and Start before growth; repair entitlement lifecycle
before price; and repair reminder timing, state, and deep links before message
copy or frequency. The first public cohorts establish product baselines. This
run does not invent retention or conversion targets without observed evidence.

## Instrumentation and metrics

[instrumentation-matrix.csv](./instrumentation-matrix.csv) assigns every metric
its events, denominator, dimensions, owner, hypothesis, decision, privacy
boundary, and current status.

### Activation coverage

- **A1 primary:** a draft open followed by a confirmed or edited first action
  and verified Start within two minutes.
- **A2 diagnostic:** recovery intent to verified Start, with elapsed time and
  stage breakdown.

A1 measures the value handoff. A2 prevents a strong draft-only rate from hiding
entry, permission, capture, local-save, processing, or ready-state loss.

### Retention coverage

- **R1 primary:** a D7 second distinct verified restart.
- **R2 derived:** D7 meaningful-return union, always split into same-thread
  progress and distinct recovery.
- **R3 diagnostic:** D1 meaningful return.
- **R4 diagnostic:** D30 second distinct verified restart.

R2 is not abandoned. The set distinguishes fast continuity, recurring need by
day seven, and longer recurrence without counting an app open as retention.

### Monetization and reminders

The monetary funnel covers paywall, dismiss, checkout, purchase, entitlement,
restore, billing issue, cancellation, renewal, refund, and expiry. No task
content or payment instrument enters product analytics.

OneSignal send, confirmed receipt, click, failure, and suppression are
diagnostics. Meaningful outcome is the app-owned verified Start on the intended
thread after a safe deep link. Current OneSignal documentation also makes a
timely evidence export important for message data with limited retention.

### RevenueCat Design — primary

**D1 Innovative ideas — tie-break critical.** Show the working sequence:
generic fast entry, durable local save, optional cloud processing, one grounded
editable action, Direct Trace-back evidence, human confirmation, Start, and a
deterministic fallback. The innovation claim becomes credible only when the
same release candidate proves that entire chain.

**D2 Aesthetics.** Show H2 Forward Thread as a state system, not decoration:
large dot, dotted context line, arrow-forward mark, tactile controls, restrained
depth, semantic motion, haptics, dark mode, scalable type, TalkBack, and a
reduced-motion static connector.

### Best App for Galaxy — conditional

The official rules place 20% of this score on Galaxy optimization, including
Samsung-specific or adaptive behavior and store quality. They do not publicly
enumerate the “standard applicable Shipaton judging criteria” that compose the
remaining 80%. The matrix therefore includes one explicit unknown row. It does
not invent criteria or a Galaxy tie-break order.

Galaxy proof requires the live US listing, polished metadata and assets,
physical or appropriately labeled Remote Test Lab evidence, adaptive continuity,
correct reading and focus order, and the required purchase lifecycle on hardware
that can prove it.

### Keep Them Coming Back — conditional

**OS1 Implementation — tie-break critical.** Prove the user-requested schedule,
message ID, current-state check, cancellation, device behavior, exact-thread
deep link, and neutral stale-target recovery.

**OS2 User value.** Measure requested reminder to verified Start. Delivery and
click data cannot substitute for the product outcome.

**OS3 Resourcefulness and creativity.** Prove state-aware suppression and safe
handling of cancellation races. Do not add an unrelated high-volume Journey to
appear advanced.

## Submission production

[submission-checklist.md](./submission-checklist.md) covers the official icon,
screenshot, video, store, English, US-access, testing, category, source,
copyright, and judge-access requirements.

The required asset minimum includes a 1024 x 1024 icon and at least one 1179 x
2556 screenshot without a device frame. The demo must be public on YouTube or
Vimeo, less than two minutes, and show the target-device app. The selected
ordinary awards do not require a public repository or open-source license; that
rule applies to Next Gen. A voluntary repository still needs secret and data
scrubbing plus an appropriate license.

### Judge-access gap

M1 intentionally has no launch trial, but Shipaton requires either a free trial
or a promo code for judges to unlock premium features. RevenueCat technically
supports immediate time-limited granted entitlements through the dashboard or a
secret server API. The recommended mechanism is an event-bound in-app judge code
that asks the backend to grant `pro` to the current anonymous RevenueCat user.

That mechanism's acceptance as the rules' “promo code” is **unknown**. Ask the
organizer. If accepted, keep the secret server-side, rate limit the code, refresh
the entitlement, and test expiry and coexistence with store billing. If rejected,
add an organizer-accepted store access route and update M1 honestly; do not
silently equate technical access with rules compliance.

## Contradictions and missing evidence

- The primary Design direction is locked, but no production UI, motion,
  physical-device frame trace, or accessibility result exists.
- The desired low-friction locked entry remains conditional. If the technical
  spike fails, the demo and listing must show the universal unlock-to-prepared-
  recorder fallback.
- The seven-cloud-draft allowance is an approved test hypothesis, not a measured
  fairness or willingness-to-pay boundary.
- M1 has no public launch trial, while the official judge-access rule requires a
  trial or promo code. RevenueCat granted entitlement makes an app code feasible,
  but organizer acceptance remains unresolved.
- Galaxy's 20% optimization criteria are visible, but the remaining 80% standard
  criteria and Galaxy-specific tie order are not.
- Samsung seller status, a live Galaxy listing, physical purchase proof, and
  adaptive evidence do not exist.
- No OneSignal campaign, App ID evidence, permission result, delivery, deep-link
  result, or verified-start outcome exists.
- No formative study or behavioral pilot has run. Predicted attention maps are
  hypotheses and cannot be called observed interaction or gaze evidence.
- No A1, A2, R1–R4, conversion, renewal, cancellation, or notification baseline
  exists. The demo must omit numerical results until their provenance exists.

## Recommendation

Approve the matrix as the release and submission proof contract, with D1 as the
first evidence priority. Keep Galaxy and OneSignal in the plan but do not enter
them merely because their SDKs are installed. Freeze their award claims unless
their matrix rows become observable in the accepted live build.

Resolve the judge-code interpretation and Galaxy 80% ambiguity with the
organizer while technical spikes are running. These questions affect compliance
and scoring clarity; they should not delay core local-save, grounding,
accessibility, purchase, and Design proof.

The UX-research guidance materially kept measures behavior-first and separated
participant statements from observed outcomes. The accessibility guidance made
the entire path—not isolated screens—the test unit and added TalkBack, switch or
voice access, semantics, 48 dp targets, focus order, scalable text, and reduced-
motion equivalence. The UX-writing guidance kept controls verb-led, errors calm
and recoverable, and cancellation questions neutral. The documentation guidance
kept the report outcome-first and separated narrative from CSV-ready proof.
