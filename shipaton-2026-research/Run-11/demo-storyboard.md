## Capture rules

- Record the working app on the target device; do not substitute Figma or a
  motion mock for a product behavior.
- Keep the cut below two minutes. Archive the exact 1:58 export and verify the
  public YouTube or Vimeo playback time.
- Capture taps, state changes, failure recovery, purchase, posture transition,
  and conditional reminder behavior visibly. Do not hide a jump behind voiceover.
- Use captions and a transcript. Do not require sound to understand the demo.
- Mask test account identifiers, notification tokens, RevenueCat App User IDs,
  OneSignal App ID if not required on screen, device serials, and all secrets.
- Show only scripted task content. Do not display a research participant's
  audio, transcript, source, or drafted action.
- Use licensed or original music only. The simplest safe option is no music.
- Film the final store build or a byte-equivalent candidate. If the store build
  changes after capture, rerun the shown path and record the parity result.

## Metric provenance card

Every numerical demo claim must have an archive record containing:

- metric definition, numerator, denominator, and observation window;
- sample size, build, platform, acquisition source when known, and observed
  date;
- source export or query and the person who verified it; and
- exclusions, missing data, and whether the result is observed or measured.

If that record does not exist, omit the number. “Not yet measured” belongs in
the research ledger, not as a disguised launch claim.
