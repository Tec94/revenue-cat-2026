# Hybrid visual feedback synthesis

## Builder decision

**H2 — Forward Thread is selected and locked for subsequent research runs.**
The earlier H1 recommendation remains useful analyst context, but later runs
must not reopen the visual direction unless testing exposes a material usability,
accessibility, similarity, or platform problem.

## Confirmed builder preferences

- Keep the A2 and A4 logo families in consideration.
- Make the A2 large dot the strongest visual focus.
- Keep a short dotted thread as the representation of preserved context.
- Use a forward arrow or notch to communicate moving ahead.
- Advance the first A2 storyboard variation: a direct connector from
  **Start here** to supporting voice-note evidence.
- Adapt the corrected A5 single-state lock-screen widget.
- Give the primary interactive action restrained A3 tactile depth.

The builder called the selected storyboard “I1 in A2.” This document records it
as **I2-V1 — Direct Trace-back Connector** to distinguish it from the original
Run 8 **I1 — Signal Settle** identifier.

## Thematic synthesis from earlier runs

The logo should communicate the product thesis rather than the capture method.

| Element | Product meaning | Evidence carried forward |
|---|---|---|
| Large dot | **Where I am now**; a stable re-entry point | C13 Reset Button surfaces one feasible restart; C14 Breadcrumb preserves the point of interruption |
| Interrupted dotted thread | Context was interrupted but not lost | The surviving wedge is context restoration, not generic AI microsteps or a planner |
| Forward arrow or notch | **The one next action I choose** | The fixed loop ends in one user-edited or confirmed action, not an autonomous plan |
| Direct evidence connector | The action has inspectable provenance | Grounded AI must cite the user's own voice note and expose assumptions and questions |
| Single-state locked widget | Capture is quick, private, and locally durable | The locked surface remains generic; local save completes before asynchronous AI |
| Restrained tactile depth | The first action is actionable and physically clear | Historical winner evidence favors complete feedback-to-result chains over decoration alone |

The mark should not encode ADHD, therapy, diagnosis, urgency, AI magic, or
gamified productivity. The emotional theme is **continuity without shame**:
the user does not start over, rebuild a plan, or earn a reward; they recover the
thread and move one verified step.

## H1 — Anchor Forward

![H1 Anchor Forward](./H1-anchor-forward-board.png)

H1 keeps A2's violet system and uses the clearest literal sequence: dotted
context enters the large present-state dot, then a forward arrow exits toward
the selected action. Its strengths are immediate product comprehension,
continuity with the evidence connector, and strong dark-mode and widget
adaptation.

Main refinement risks:

- Multiple incoming branches can resemble sharing, a graph, or model routing.
  The final mark should use one interrupted path unless testing proves a branch
  is clearer.
- A freestanding arrow can resemble generic navigation. Join it more tightly to
  the dot or cut it as a forward notch.
- The primary action can keep tactile depth, but other surfaces should remain
  nearly flat.

## H2 — Forward Thread

![H2 Forward Thread](./H2-forward-thread-board.png)

H2 keeps A4's editorial confidence. The open bracket acknowledges an interrupted
thought, the large dot provides a stable present point, and the forward arrow
makes the next move explicit. Its strongest qualities are static recognition,
store presentation, and a more assertive brand voice.

Main refinement risks:

- The open bracket can resemble a crop, scan, focus, quotation, or camera mark.
  A final version should simplify the boundary and test recognition without the
  wordmark.
- The separated triangle can read as media play or browser navigation. A notch
  cut from the boundary is safer than a detached triangle.
- Serif display type should remain marketing-only if it slows the critical
  in-app scan path.

## Pre-selection analyst recommendation

Advance **H1 Anchor Forward** as the product-system baseline, but test a version
where H2's forward notch is cut directly into H1's right edge. That retains the
large-dot and dotted-thread meaning while reducing the generic freestanding
arrow. Keep H2 as the bolder store-presentation comparison.

The next deterministic design step is a vector logo sheet that compares:

1. one dotted path → large dot → integrated forward notch;
2. large dot inside one open boundary → integrated forward notch;
3. minimal two-dot approach → large dot → notch for small icon sizes.

Those marks should be tested in monochrome, Android themed icons, 48 px and
smaller contexts, and the generic lock-screen state before choosing the final
logo.
