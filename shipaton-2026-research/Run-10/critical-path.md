## Why September 8 is the recommended first store date

The official rules require a fully published eligible-store app by September
30 and warn that review can take multiple days or more. Google adds a hard
14-day closed-test prerequisite for new personal accounts and then a separate
production-access review. Samsung adds commercial seller and physical purchase
dependencies. September 8 is therefore an owner-selected early submission
target that preserves time to answer Galaxy review while the Google gate runs
in parallel. It is not an assertion that Samsung will approve the app by a
particular date.

## First three technical spikes

### Spike 1 — durable capture and locked-entry truth

Prove on physical Android and a physical Galaxy that a user-visible action can
start prepared capture, stop it, durably save it, survive interruption and
process death, and expose no content on a locked surface. Test app shortcut,
home widget, notification action, and any Samsung host that is actually
available. Outcome: one supported entry promise or the universal unlock-to-
prepared-recorder fallback.

### Spike 2 — two-store purchase truth

Compile Play and Galaxy flavors. Fetch the same `default` Offering, make and
fail a test purchase, restore, and observe expiry or grace state. Galaxy proof
must use a physical Galaxy signed into a Samsung account. Outcome: keep both
stores, or remove Galaxy billing and its secondary award without touching the
core app.

### Spike 3 — grounded cloud recovery truth

Send one accepted 60-second sample through the Supabase Edge Function to one
provider. Measure upload, transcription, generation, total latency, cost,
retry, and failure; validate citation coverage and reversed-negation fixtures;
inspect every log for content leakage. Outcome: ship grounded AI, revise the
bounded path, or release the deterministic recovery without a cloud claim.
