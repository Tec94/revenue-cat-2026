# Run 5 — disciplined concept generation

Run 5 produces 21 bounded concepts across the seven selected problem
statements. Six concepts remain strong under every weighting model. The
recommended adversarial-research portfolio is C01 TraceTag, C04 ScopeSignal,
and C14 Breadcrumb. This is a recommendation, not a concept decision.

## Executive summary

The portfolio preserves the historical winner pattern of a narrow first wedge,
a coherent primary loop, short time to value, judge-observable proof, and
monetization after delivered value. It does not assume that AI, platform APIs,
or award count predict winning.

- C01 TraceTag ranks first in the base, user-value, and commercial models. It
  converts inspector field evidence into a human-verified report-ready bundle.
- C14 Breadcrumb ranks first in the platform-and-award model and ties for first
  in the user-value model. It preserves context across interruptions without
  becoming another planner.
- C13 Reset Button ranks first in the solo-builder model. It is the simplest
  high-evidence fallback but is less differentiated than C14.
- C02 FieldBoard is the cleanest Galaxy-primary concept. It removes model risk
  and makes foldable, tablet, multi-window, and DeX behavior part of the core.
- C04 ScopeSignal is the strongest recipient-side commercial concept. Its main
  unknown is whether clients will correct and confirm an external link.
- C16 Capacity Compass is the highest-ranked impact concept, but safety language
  and community co-design are mandatory because digital pacing efficacy is not
  established.

The selected platform is now **H2A: Android-first shared core**. Android ships
and stabilizes first. iOS and iPadOS reuse the shared product layer second. The
initial Mac path is the compatible iPad or iPhone build on Apple-silicon Macs,
not a native macOS or unsupported Catalyst promise.

## Research update

Run 5 used the 61-source Run 4 ledger as inherited evidence and added 22
concept- and platform-specific records. Exa supplied query variation,
discovery, and full-text extraction. Firecrawl performed two documented
dynamic-page validations: the live Devpost rules and the Attik Featurebase
inspection request. No browser or vision fallback was needed.

The incremental evidence materially changed four decisions:

- A current inspection workflow request explicitly describes double entry of
  defect location from photos and spoken narrative, while a field review
  describes the cost of placing photos during capture. This supports C01 and
  C02 but also shows that incumbents are moving quickly.
- Household research continues to support complete cognitive ownership and
  warns that adoption by the less-engaged partner is the unresolved barrier.
  This eliminates administrator-heavy chore concepts.
- ADHD research supports initiation, interruption, emotional-regulation, and
  social-scaffold mechanisms. It does not validate an AI planner, so C13 and
  C14 remain deterministic.
- Chronic-fatigue research supports individualized, conservative pacing while
  reporting no superiority over usual care in one six-month digital trial.
  T5-S2 concepts therefore avoid efficacy, diagnosis, and threshold claims.

## Recommended adversarial-research portfolio

The recommended three concepts maximize information value across a professional
field workflow, a recipient-side commercial loop, and a personal return loop.
They do not share a persona or core loop and must remain separate products.

### C01 TraceTag

TraceTag is the strongest overall commercial hypothesis and makes judge proof
obvious: capture loose field evidence, verify the inferred context, and export a
defensible artifact. Adversarial research must test whether inspectors will use
a companion instead of an incumbent suite, how often location association is
wrong, and whether human confirmation remains faster than manual entry.

### C04 ScopeSignal

ScopeSignal tests recipient-side value and natural distribution. The client is
not merely an acquisition target; correction protects both parties. Adversarial
research must test whether clients open and correct the link, whether the tone
feels collaborative rather than legalistic, and whether users will pay before a
dispute occurs.

### C14 Breadcrumb

Breadcrumb is more differentiated and platform-native than a general reset
planner. It creates a 20-second before-or-after interruption loop with visible
Android and Galaxy leverage. Adversarial research must test whether users
remember to leave the breadcrumb, whether it measurably shortens resumption,
and whether capture remains private and low effort.

C02, C13, and C16 are the strongest alternatives. C02 is the safer non-AI
inspection concept; C13 is the easiest build; C16 is the strongest impact route.
Selecting both C01 and C02 would concentrate two of three research slots on the
same inspector market. Selecting both C13 and C14 is defensible only if the user
wants to test a future combined resumption loop.

does not provide an equivalent installation and physical-device test guide.
This contradiction requires an immediate spike.

## Platform implementation hypothesis

H2A uses shared product code without pretending every capability is portable.
The stack remains a hypothesis until one concept is selected.

1. Build the responsive product shell, local data model, core loop, error states,
   analytics events, and paywall state in a shared TypeScript application.
2. Wrap Android first with Capacitor and implement only the native bridges the
   selected loop needs, such as share targets, widgets, quick settings, camera,
   filesystem, notifications, fold state, or Galaxy billing.
3. Validate phone, resizable window, tablet, foldable, multi-window, rotation,
   process death, offline recovery, keyboard, and screen-reader behavior before
   adding a Samsung-specific flourish.
4. Add iOS and iPadOS after the Android core and RevenueCat entitlement states
   are stable. Use MacinCloud for Xcode build, TestFlight, and store work.
5. Make the iPad or iPhone build available on Apple-silicon Macs only after
   compatibility testing. Do not market it as a native Mac app.

The first technical spike must test the current RevenueCat Capacitor SDK against
a Galaxy build. Native Galaxy purchases require an additional RevenueCat module
and a physical Galaxy device signed into a Samsung account; the emulator is not
sufficient. If the spike fails:

- drop the Galaxy secondary award for C01 or C14;
- use a small, isolated native Capacitor bridge if the maintenance cost is
  acceptable; or
- reconsider native Android or React Native only if C02 remains the selected
  Galaxy-primary concept.

This sequence keeps iOS and Mac as real secondary delivery targets without
putting three platform toolchains on the critical path from day one.

## AI discipline

No concept is a generic AI assistant. Model inference appears only where a
specific transformation cannot be achieved economically with simple rules.

- C01 can use speech and image context to propose a location, but a human must
  confirm it and the source remains visible.
- C03 can parse a report and propose evidence gaps, but it cannot issue a legal
  or professional compliance score.
- C04, C05, and C06 can transcribe and extract source-linked changes, briefs, or
  decisions. The sender and recipient correction states remain authoritative.
- C19 can generate a short diagnostic only from user-supplied source material
  with citations and a manual “the map is wrong” path.
- All household, caregiver, restart, capacity, and backlog concepts use explicit
  user input and deterministic logic at launch.

Every model-enabled concept must instrument input size, latency, correction
rate, rejection rate, cost per completed value event, and deletion. A high
correction rate is counterevidence, not engagement.

## Contradictions and missing evidence

The following gaps block a final concept or stack decision.

- No primary discovery interview or observed usability test exists for any of
  the seven selected segments.
- The top inspector concepts face active incumbent development in the same
  photo, voice, and location workflow. Differentiation may shrink quickly.
- Product-vendor sources support a workflow but cannot establish prevalence,
  independent time savings, legal effect, or revenue.
- Household ownership tools cannot create partner willingness. Product evidence
  must distinguish voluntary adoption from compliance under pressure.
- Caregiver concepts require threat modeling, least-privilege testing, account
  recovery, and legal review before storing sensitive information.
- ADHD studies are qualitative, a preprint, or an exploratory thesis; none prove
  that C13, C14, or C15 improves daily functioning.
- Digital pacing evidence is mixed. T5-S2 concepts can support user-authored
  reflection and planning but cannot promise symptom prevention.
- T5-S3 evidence is strongest for Anki-style review backlogs and weaker for the
  broad long-horizon learner population.
- Capacitor officially targets Android, iOS, and web. Dedicated macOS and Mac
  Catalyst are unsupported by Capacitor and excluded from launch scope.
- RevenueCat's current Galaxy documentation conflicts on whether Capacitor has a
  complete supported billing path. The Galaxy award is conditional on a spike.
- Galaxy test purchases require a physical Galaxy device signed into a Samsung
  account. The builder has not confirmed access to either.
- Apple, Google Play, and Samsung developer accounts are not confirmed active.
  Store setup and review can consume the remaining schedule.
- Scores, build bands, correction rates, conversion, retention, and user
  willingness to pay remain analyst hypotheses until measured.

## Recommendation

Advance C01 TraceTag, C04 ScopeSignal, and C14 Breadcrumb to Run 6. This trio
tests three different demand and operational profiles while preserving the
Android-first strategy. Run 6 must try to disprove each concept through primary
recruiting, hands-on competitor tests, policy review, technical spikes, and
prototype tasks before any final concept selection.

Keep C02 FieldBoard as the fallback if model-assisted inspection capture fails,
C13 Reset Button as the fallback if breadcrumb capture compliance is poor, and
C16 Capacity Compass as the impact alternative only after community and expert
review.
