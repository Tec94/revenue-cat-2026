## Design Context

### Users

Restart Thread serves US adults who self-identify with ADHD, time blindness,
interruption sensitivity, or recurring difficulty resuming work. It is used in
the disorienting moment after context is lost: the user needs to speak or type
what is true, preserve it privately, and return to one grounded first action
without needing a diagnosis or encountering treatment claims.

### Brand Personality

Editorial, calm, and physically decisive. The emotional promise is continuity
without shame: an interrupted thread is recoverable, the user's own words are
evidence, and the user—not the model—chooses what happens next.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the locked H2 Forward Thread system: warm paper and dense ink, one editorial
red, a dominant present-state dot, interrupted context dots, a forward arrow,
serif display moments, quiet system-sans controls, and tactile depth reserved
for the primary action. Support light, dark, increased contrast, scalable text,
reduced motion, and adaptive phone/fold/tablet layouts. Do not resemble a
wellness gradient, generic AI assistant, emergency app, task-board game, or
streak product; avoid neon, glass, sparkles, confetti, diagnostic imagery,
shame language, and reward mechanics.

### Design Principles

1. Save locally before showing success, requesting cloud work, or producing a
   recovery draft.
2. Make capture immediate and keep one editable, user-confirmed action as the
   strongest decision on screen.
3. Preserve provenance through the Direct Trace-back relationship between the
   proposed action and unchanged source evidence.
4. Share the core information architecture and visual language across
   platforms, while permissions, recording, secure storage, widgets, billing,
   haptics, notifications, and system navigation remain platform-native.
5. Keep locked surfaces generic and private; never expose transcript, task, or
   proposed-action content before unlock.
